What to bring

Your application, in one visit.

  • Your secondary-school certificate (the General Secondary Certificate or an equivalent) and your final grade.

  • Your national ID number, or your passport if you are applying from abroad.
  • An email address and a phone number, so we can keep you updated on your application.
