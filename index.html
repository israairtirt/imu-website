International recognition

Recognised internationally through NCC Education.

NCC Education is a British awarding body whose qualifications are regulated in the UK by Ofqual. Through NCC, our students can go on to earn their degree from a British university.

Awarding body and regulator

Degrees from British universities

Huawei ICT Academy

The college is a Huawei ICT Academy partner: our students can take Huawei's courses, and get vouchers for its certification exams, free of charge.

Admissions

Applying, step by step.

  1. 1

    Choose a programme

    Look through our programmes, or ask the admissions office which one suits you.

  2. 2

    Visit the admissions office

    Bring your secondary-school certificate and your ID, and we register your application on the spot.

  3. 3

    Get our decision

    The admissions office reviews your application and lets you know. You can check its progress online at any time.

  4. 4

    Start your studies

    Once you are accepted, you enrol for your first semester and start your classes.
